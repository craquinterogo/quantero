# References and Resources

## Classic Books

### Portfolio Theory and Asset Pricing
- **"Portfolio Selection"** by Harry Markowitz (1952)
  - Foundation of Modern Portfolio Theory
  
- **"Capital Ideas"** by Peter Bernstein
  - History of modern finance theory
  
- **"A Random Walk Down Wall Street"** by Burton Malkiel
  - Accessible introduction to efficient markets

### Derivatives
- **"Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives"** by John Hull
  - Comprehensive derivatives textbook
  
- **"Dynamic Hedging"** by Nassim Taleb
  - Practical trading and risk management
  
- **"The Concepts and Practice of Mathematical Finance"** by Mark Joshi
  - Mathematical rigor for practitioners

### Risk Management
- **"Value at Risk"** by Philippe Jorion
  - Comprehensive VaR guide
  
- **"The Black Swan"** by Nassim Taleb
  - Impact of rare events
  
- **"Against the Gods"** by Peter Bernstein
  - History of risk management

### Fixed Income
- **"Fixed Income Securities"** by Bruce Tuckman and Angel Serrat
  - Modern fixed income analysis
  
- **"Bond Markets, Analysis and Strategies"** by Frank Fabozzi
  - Comprehensive bond market guide

### Quantitative Methods
- **"Quantitative Finance"** by Paul Wilmott
  - Comprehensive quant finance textbook
  
- **"Analysis of Financial Time Series"** by Ruey Tsay
  - Econometric methods for finance
  
- **"Elements of Statistical Learning"** by Hastie, Tibshirani, Friedman
  - Machine learning foundations

### Algorithmic Trading
- **"Algorithmic Trading"** by Ernie Chan
  - Practical quantitative trading strategies
  
- **"Advances in Financial Machine Learning"** by Marcos López de Prado
  - Modern ML techniques for trading
  
- **"Quantitative Trading"** by Ernie Chan
  - Strategy development and backtesting
